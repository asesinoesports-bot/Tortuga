# Arise Scrims Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** August 2026

## 1. Introduction

Arise Scrims ("the Bot") is a Discord application developed to manage competitive esports scrims and events. This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected, why it is collected, how it is used, and the choices available to users.

By using Arise Scrims, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

---

## 2. Information We Collect

To provide its functionality, Arise Scrims may collect and process:

- Discord User ID
- Discord Server (Guild) ID
- Discord Role IDs
- Discord Channel IDs
- Team Name
- Team Identifier
- Registration information submitted through bot commands or forms
- Event and scrim participation records
- Group assignments
- Workflow execution state
- Operational logs necessary for reliability and crash recovery

### Arise Scrims does **NOT** collect:

- Private Direct Messages (DMs)
- Voice communications
- Images or files uploaded outside bot interactions
- Message history unrelated to bot commands
- User passwords
- Payment information

---

## 3. How We Use Data

Collected information is used exclusively to:

- Register teams
- Manage scrims and events
- Assign and remove Discord roles
- Generate schedules
- Track event participation
- Synchronize registrations
- Recover workflows after unexpected failures
- Improve service reliability

---

## 4. Storage

Operational data may be stored using:

- Firebase Realtime Database
- SQLite
- Google Sheets (for event management only)

Only information required for bot functionality is stored.

---

## 5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade user information.

Information is never shared with advertisers.

Information is only processed through trusted infrastructure required for operation.

---

## 6. Data Security

Reasonable administrative and technical safeguards are implemented to protect stored information.

While no internet service can guarantee absolute security, we continuously work to maintain secure systems.

---

## 7. Data Retention

Operational data is retained only as long as required for:

- Active events
- Recovery processes
- Administrative records

Users may request deletion of their data.

---

## 8. User Rights

Users may request:

- Data deletion
- Correction of stored information
- Information regarding stored data

Requests can be made through the official Arise Scrims support server.

---

## 9. Children's Privacy

The Bot is not directed toward children under the age required by Discord's Terms of Service.

---

## 10. Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically.

Continued use of Arise Scrims constitutes acceptance of any revisions.
